# How A Bill Becomes A Law 

## What is a Bill?


 - All Laws in the US bgin as bills
 
 - The idea for these may come from a representative or from a citizen.

 
![The Bill](Images/thebill.png)


## 4 Basic Steps 

### **Introduction**

A Formal proposal is made before the House or the Senate

![How A Bill Becomes A Law](Images/Howabillbecomesalaw.jpg)
__________________________________________________________

### **Committee Review**
- A Subcommittee hearing is held to "mark up the bill"
- The bill is then sent to the "full committee" where they vote on the bill
__________________________________________________________

### **Committee Actions**
When a subcommittee complete its work,the bill returns to the full committe.The full committee may then:
1. Report on the bill favorably,with a "do pass" recommendation
1. Report on the bill with an unfavorable recommendation
1. Report a committee bill
1. Report on the bill in an amendment form
1. Refuse to report on a bill
__________________________________________________________

### **Rules Committee**
- Decides when bills will be heard and whether or not they are open or close for amendments.
__________________________________________________________

### **Floor Debate and Passage**
- House of Representatives has strict rules for debate
- Time limits:usually each speaker is only allotted 5 minutes to speak and debate in total is limited to an house
- speakers are approved ahead of time
- poeple who offer an amendment must be approved ahead of time
- Senate has very few rules for debate
- Bill must pass in each chamber
- If the bill is different in one house to the next then goes to "conference committee"
- A single joint committee made up of memebers from both the House and the Senate.
___________________________________________________________

### **Conference Committee**
- The newer version again must go back to each house and get passed.

___________________________________________________________

### **Presential Approval**
- <u>President Signs</u>
or..
- <u>President Vetoes</u> or..
- <u>President does nothing</u>..

```
In 10 days,if congress is still in session,the bill becomes a law


In 10 days,if congress is adjoured,the bill does not become a law,known as a "pocket veto"
```

- If the President does veto the bill, it's sent back to both houses where it will require a **2/3rd majority to override**.
- Very difficult to override because the president can usually muster up enough support in his own party to prevent a 2/3rds vote.

## Ways to Kill A Bill

1. The House subcommittee and its chair
1. The chair of the House stadning committee
1. The house standing committe and its leaders.
1. The House Rules committee
1. The majority of the House.
1. The senate subcommittee
1. The Senate standing committee.
1. The majority of the Senate.
1. The floor leaders in both chambers.
1. Senators can filibuster or talk to delay a vote
1. The House-Senate conference committee (if chambers disagree)
1. The President (veto)
